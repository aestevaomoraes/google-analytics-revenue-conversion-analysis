
# 📊 Business Findings

## Executive Summary

The analysis explored revenue generation and conversion behavior using Google Analytics session data stored in BigQuery.

A total of 1,711 sessions were analyzed, resulting in 34 purchasing sessions and a conversion rate of 1.99%.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Conversion Rate

- Total Sessions: 1,711
- Purchasing Sessions: 34
- Conversion Rate: 1.99%

**Business Insight**

Only a small percentage of sessions generated revenue, highlighting the importance of optimizing acquisition channels and user journeys.

---

### 2. Homepage as a Revenue Driver

The homepage was the primary landing page among sessions that generated revenue.

**Business Insight**

The homepage plays a critical role in guiding users toward conversion.

---

### 3. Traffic Volume Does Not Equal Conversion

The channel with the highest traffic volume was not the channel with the highest conversion rate.

**Business Insight**

Marketing investments should be evaluated based on conversion efficiency, not only traffic volume.

---

### 4. Device Performance

Desktop users generated nearly all purchasing sessions.

**Business Insight**

The desktop experience appears to be significantly more effective for conversions than mobile and tablet devices.

---

### 5. User Engagement

Medium-engagement users generated more revenue than high-engagement users.

**Business Insight**

Longer engagement does not necessarily translate into higher revenue generation.

---

### 6. Referral Traffic Quality

Referral traffic achieved the highest conversion rate among analyzed channels.

**Business Insight**

Partnerships and referral sources may represent high-quality acquisition opportunities.

---

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how BigQuery SQL techniques such as CTEs, COUNTIF(), ARRAY_LENGTH(), STRUCT, ARRAY, and UNNEST() can transform nested web analytics data into actionable business insights.
